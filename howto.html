How to install?

Welcome! This page will guide you through installing the app and explain our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use in clear, simple language.

1. Download the App

a. Tap the download link for the latest version of the app (APK file). Example: `Download App v1.0.0 (APK)`

b. Your browser may ask you to confirm the download. Tap 'Download' or 'OK' to continue.

2. Allow Install from This Source (Android Only)

Because this app is not from the Google Play Store, Android may block the installation at first. To fix this:

a. When you open the APK, you may see a message like: “For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source.”

b. Tap 'Settings' (or 'Allow from this source').

c. Turn on the option to allow installs from your browser or file manager.

d. Go back, then tap 'Install' again.

Note: The exact wording and steps may be slightly different depending on your Android version and device brand.

3. Install the APK

a. Find the downloaded APK file:

b. Your browser may ask you to confirm the download. Tap 'Download' or 'OK' to continue.

c. Tap the APK file.

d. When the install screen appears, tap 'Install'.

4. Updating the App

When a new version is released:

a. Download the new APK from the same link or page.

b. Open the downloaded APK.

c. Tap 'Install'. In most cases, it will update the existing app without removing your data (if any).
