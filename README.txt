# How to Install

Welcome! This page will guide you through installing the app and explain our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use in clear, simple language.

---

## 1. Download the App

1. Tap the download link for the latest version of the app (APK file).  
   Example: `Download App v1.0.0 (APK)`

2. Your browser may ask you to confirm the download.  
   Tap **Download** or **OK** to continue.

---

## 2. Allow Install from This Source (Android Only)

Because this app is not from the Google Play Store, Android may block the installation at first. To fix this:

1. When you open the APK, you may see a message like:  
   “For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source.”

2. Tap **Settings** (or **Allow from this source**).

3. Turn on the option to allow installs from your browser or file manager.

4. Go back, then tap **Install** again.

> Note: The exact wording and steps may be slightly different depending on your Android version and device brand.

---

## 3. Install the APK

1. Find the downloaded APK file:
   - Open your **Downloads** folder in your browser, or
   - Open the **Files** / **My Files** app and go to **Downloads**.

2. Tap the APK file.

3. When the install screen appears, tap **Install**.

4. Wait for the installation to finish, then tap **Open** to start using the app.

---

## 4. Updating the App

When a new version is released:

1. Download the new APK from the same link or page.
2. Open the downloaded APK.
3. Tap **Install**.  
   In most cases, it will update the existing app without removing your data (if any).
