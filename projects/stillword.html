Stillword (v1.0.1) — Random Bible Verse Generator

Stillword is a minimal Bible verse generator designed for people who want quick spiritual encouragement without the clutter of traditional Bible apps. With a single button, it surfaces a truly random Bible verse, and from there, users can choose to open and read the entire chapter that verse belongs to.
